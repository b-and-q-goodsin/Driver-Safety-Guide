Voice recorder

For the native speaker who is recording the safety page in their own language.

Nothing is uploaded. Recordings stay on this phone until you save and send them.
  1. Find a quiet room. Hold the phone about 15 cm from your mouth.
  2. Choose your language below.
  3. For each page: tap Record, read the text aloud slowly and clearly, exactly as written, then tap Stop.
  4. Tap Play to check it. Tap Record again to redo it.
  5. Tap Save. Do this for all 6 pages, then send the 6 saved files to the person who asked you.
